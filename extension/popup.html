Place a call

Enter a number and press the button. Your paired Android phone places the call from its SIM.

Use a country code when possible. No call is sent until you press the button.

i

Calls use your phone’s SIM and mobile plan. Your carrier may charge for the call.

Connection & pairing

Paste the deployed Google Apps Script URL ending in /exec.

This must exactly match the Device ID in the Android app.

Settings are stored in this Chrome profile. The same relay URL, secret and device ID must be entered in Auto Caller on Android.
